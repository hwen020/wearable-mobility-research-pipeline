      Research methods
      
      
      
      
        
        
      • Daily distance sums Haversine segments within each study day. Routes split on gaps over two hours and speeds above 120 km/h.
        • 
        
      • Anchor-radius stay detection uses 120 m, a 15-minute minimum, and contiguous observations.
        • 
        
      • Activity area is the convex hull of 100 m buffers in a local projected CRS. GeoJSON coordinates are WGS84 longitude and latitude.
        • 
        
      • Observed Validity Rate uses accepted GPS rows and excludes rows that end speed-flagged segments.
        • 
        
      • Expected Sampling Coverage counts expected slots containing at least one valid observation. N/A means no observation window applies.
        • 
        
      • Daily heart rate is the mean of available values. Steps are per-interval counts. Results describe simulated trajectories.
